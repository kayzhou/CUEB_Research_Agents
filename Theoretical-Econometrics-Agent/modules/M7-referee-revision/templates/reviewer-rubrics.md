# Reviewer Rubrics

## Theoretical econometrics referee

Questions:

1. Is the estimator clearly defined?
2. Is the parameter space compact and meaningful?
3. Is identification credible?
4. Is the asymptotic regime explicit?
5. Are assumptions minimal or excessive?
6. Are theorem statements precise?
7. Does the paper make a genuine theoretical contribution?

## Spatial econometrics expert

Questions:

1. Is the spatial weights matrix valid?
2. Is the spatial multiplier invertible?
3. Is the log-determinant term correct?
4. Is the model SAR, SDM, SEM, SAC, or another class?
5. Are direct/indirect effects relevant?
6. Does the simulation use realistic spatial dependence?

## Mathematical proof auditor

Questions:

1. Are stochastic orders consistent?
2. Is uniform convergence actually shown?
3. Is the break objective drift separated from noise?
4. Is the Taylor expansion justified?
5. Is the information matrix nonsingular?
6. Are all lemmas proved or cited?

## MATLAB simulation auditor

Questions:

1. Does the DGP equal the theoretical model?
2. Are dimensions correct?
3. Are seeds reproducible?
4. Are failures reported?
5. Are bias/RMSE/CP formulas correct?
6. Are tables generated from raw results?

## Exposition editor

Questions:

1. Is the contribution clear?
2. Are equations readable?
3. Is notation consistent?
4. Are assumptions understandable?
5. Does the appendix match the main text?
